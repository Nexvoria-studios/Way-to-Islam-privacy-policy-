Last updated: August 30, 2026
Privacy at a glance: Way to Islam does not intentionally collect, sell, or share your personal information with us or third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

1. Information We Collect

Way to Islam does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or password.

We do not intentionally collect or store your personal information on our own servers.

2. Location Information

Way to Islam may request access to your device's location to provide location-based Islamic features, such as prayer times and Qibla direction.

Your location is used to provide these features. We do not intentionally collect or store your precise location on our own servers. You can enable or disable location permission at any time through your device settings.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Some app preferences and settings may be stored locally on your device so the application can remember your choices. This information is not intentionally transmitted to us.

4. Internet and Third-Party Services

Some features may require an internet connection or use third-party services to retrieve information necessary for the app to function. Those services may process technical information required to provide their service.

We do not intentionally send personal information to third parties unless it is necessary for a feature you request or required by applicable law.

5. Advertising and Analytics

Way to Islam does not intentionally use your personal information for personalized advertising or sell your personal information to advertisers.

If advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or other third-party services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Way to Islam is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users through the application.

8. Third-Party Links

The application may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before using them.
